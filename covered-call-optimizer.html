Assessment process
1. Load the saved Questrade / Yahoo capture and original transaction records.
2. Recalculate the optimizer at the chart’s stock price and apply each Close / Expire choice. Close today stays at captured quotes.
3. Send verified USD cash flows, profit, funding requirements and assumptions to your configured OpenAI model.
4. Save its assessment and input snapshot in SQLite. No trade is placed; no live news or account cash balance is supplied.

Close: sell stock and close the replacement call at the model horizon. Expire: assume the replacement expires worthless and retain the stock. The existing-call buyback is charged in both modes.
Sale floor = original stock purchase price × 1.10.
Close profit = closing cash + original premium − original stock cost.
Expire option P&L = original premium + new premium − held-call buyback today.
Path percentages describe this simulation, not guaranteed probabilities. Early assignment is not simulated.
Roll cash needed = max(existing-call buyback − new premium, $0), before fees. Assumes a combined rollover; executing legs separately may require the full buyback amount first.
