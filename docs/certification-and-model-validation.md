Research date: November 3, 2026. Program terms and fees should be rechecked before enrollment or procurement.

## App assurance and professional credentials

For aiMoney, the recommended starting point is an independent model-validation report. An independent quantitative-finance reviewer should assess pricing, transaction accounting, assignment and retained-share treatment, simulation assumptions, data quality, and performance on unseen data. Passing software tests verifies implementation behavior; predictive validity needs separate evidence.

OSFI's revised E-23 model-risk guideline provides a useful institutional benchmark for conceptual soundness, performance evaluation and independent review. It applies to federally regulated financial institutions and takes effect May 1, 2027. It is not a certification program for aiMoney. [OSFI E-23](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/guideline-e-23-model-risk-management-2027)

| Option | Applies to | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Independent model validation | Financial models | External assessment of correctness, assumptions, limitations and performance evidence; recommended first step. |
| SOC 2 Type II report | Service organization's controls | CPA attestation covering relevant controls over a period; useful for institutional procurement. Not a product certification. |
| ISO/IEC 27001 certification | Organization's information-security management system | Security governance and risk management, within the certificate's defined scope. |
| CQF | Individual | Practical quantitative finance, derivatives, modelling and programming. |
| FRM | Individual | Financial risk management, valuation and risk models. |
| CFA charter | Individual | Investment analysis, valuation, portfolio management and ethics. |

Sources: [AICPA SOC services](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services), [ISO/IEC 27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001), [GARP FRM requirements](https://www.garp.org/frm/frequently-asked-questions), [CFA charter requirements](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/charter).

Professional credentials qualify individuals; they do not approve an app or validate its forecasts. Security assurance also does not demonstrate investment performance. Suggested sequence: document and independently validate the model, then pursue security assurance according to target customers' procurement requirements.

Commercial personalized securities recommendations also warrant a Canadian registration assessment. Whether registration or an exemption applies depends on the activities and business model. [CSA registration guidance](https://www.securities-administrators.ca/registration/)

## CQF: what it is

The Certificate in Quantitative Finance is a professional qualification delivered by Fitch Learning and awarded by the CQF Institute. It is advertised at master's level; it is not a university master's degree. It develops practical quantitative-finance skills rather than certifying a particular app. [CQF overview](https://www.cqf.com/about-cqf/program-structure/what-is-cqf)

The curriculum comprises quantitative-finance foundations, risk and return, equities and currencies, two data-science/machine-learning modules, and fixed income/credit, plus two advanced electives. Its implementation emphasis includes Python labs. [Program structure](https://www.cqf.com/about)

## Format, preparation and assessment

- Online, part-time; a six-month full program or two levels across cohorts.
- Provider's normal weekly guidance: two 2.5-hour lectures plus approximately 10 hours of additional study, around 15 hours total. Individual needs vary.
- Included optional primers cover mathematics, Python and finance; preparation time is additional.
- Three take-home, open-book exams after modules 2, 3 and 4; each allows two weeks and requires 60% to pass.
- A supervised practical final project, selected from approved topics, and an optional distinction examination.
- A three-year completion window and ongoing access to alumni learning resources.

Sources: [FAQ](https://www.cqf.com/frequently-asked-questions), [assessment](https://www.cqf.com/about-cqf/program-delivery/assessment), [program overview](https://www.cqf.com/about-cqf/program-structure/program-overview).

## Company-funded training and B.C. support

The user confirmed B.C. residency and corrected the funding arrangement: tuition would be funded by their own company, as a self-employed business owner, rather than paid personally.

The **B.C. Employer Training Grant (ETG)** is a potential funding route. WorkBC explicitly permits self-employed business owners to apply, naming themselves as both applicant employer and participant. This does not establish that the company, participant or chosen course meets every eligibility requirement. [WorkBC FAQ](https://www.workbc.ca/employers-industry/funding-programs/bc-employer-training-grant/frequently-asked-questions)

- Reimburses **80% of eligible training costs, capped at C$10,000 per participant per fiscal year**; the company pays costs upfront and claims reimbursement.
- Applications must be submitted **before training starts**. WorkBC encourages applying at least 60 days beforehand; the outcome can be approval, denial or return without assessment.
- Training must meet the program's employment-outcome requirements. Explain how the proposed skills support the business's work developing and commercializing aiMoney, rather than assuming technical relevance alone qualifies.
- Degree and diploma programs are excluded, in full or in part. A certificate or individual course still requires assessment; do not assume any offering above is funded.

Sources: [ETG program details](https://www.workbc.ca/employers-industry/funding-programs/bc-employer-training-grant), [application and eligibility FAQ](https://www.workbc.ca/employers-industry/funding-programs/bc-employer-training-grant/frequently-asked-questions).
