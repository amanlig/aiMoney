## Deployed

- **Close today midpoint valuation:** stock midpoint proceeds less held-call
  midpoint buyback. Captured stock/option bid and ask remain unchanged in the
  actual Close workflow; actual execution prices remain manual. Midpoint fills
  are not guaranteed. Slider-close estimates retain bid/ask assumptions.
- **Date entry:** explicit YYYY-MM-DD keyboard segments. Tab moves year → month
  → day; Shift+Tab moves back. Calendar selection, pasted dates and validation
  remain available across the React transaction forms.
- **Interest rate:** Profile → Settings defaults to a Treasury par yield matched
  to the held call's remaining maturity, with interpolation and a manual override.
  The source, observation date and maturity are visible. The application converts
  the semiannual bond-equivalent yield to a continuous rate and passes `rate=`
  explicitly to model evaluation and option repricing. The same scalar rate is
  used for replacement calls and discounting; this simplification is labeled.
  Preferences are saved per browser. Snapshots, recommendations and CSV retain
  rate provenance. Treasury retrieval uses a cache and explicit unavailable-data
  handling, with no silent fixed-4% fallback in the application.
- **Rollover form:** settlement date and fees clear after a successful save;
  failed saves preserve entries.
- **Transaction report headings:** numeric/dollar columns align right; text
  columns align left.
